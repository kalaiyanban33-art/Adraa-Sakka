Cooked the way it was taught to us

Adraa Sakka began with a simple idea: a restaurant should taste like the kitchen of someone who loves feeding people. We build every plate from scratch, from the masala base to the last curry leaf.

The dining room is warm wood and soft light, made for long lunches, family dinners and quiet celebrations.

Spices ground in-houseWhole spices, roasted and ground each morning.
Cooked to orderNo shortcuts, so please allow time for the good stuff.
Family-friendlyGroup tables, high chairs and mild options for kids.
